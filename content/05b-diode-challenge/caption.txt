See the little recess between the switch openings? That was for one diode. We had 1 mm between the PCB and the plate, and the diode stood a little taller. Our first drawing called for machining that recess 22 times per half.

We ended up moving the diodes round to the back of the PCB. In the second slide you can see one beside a hotswap socket. That move lets us cut the whole plate from flat 1.2 mm polycarbonate.
