# Between the build posts

The early Ergogen keymap images in `01-layout` and `13-zmk` document a
layout study. For publishing current controls, use the
[firmware keymap](../spec/keymap.md). The render feet and short encoder
shaft are visual studies outside the sample BOM.


**One pocket per diode.** Ready to post after 05. Use the two cards in `05b-diode-challenge`. Let people see the old plate pocket and guess before swiping. For a Story, share the first card and ask “Which part would you change?” with “Plate” and “PCB” as the poll choices. Share the second after voting.

**Will this cable fit?** An optional follow-up to 04. Frame a real USB-C plug head-on beside the case opening. Ask “Which part hits first?” On the next slide show the same plug from the side, seated as far as it goes. Get the shot on the first article with the controller installed. Caption the actual result and identify the plug tested. The housing around the metal tip is the detail worth seeing.

**Where does the plate actually rest?** Pair with 05 as a Story. Shoot one perimeter tab from above, then from the side with the cover lifted so the PORON pads are visible. Use the same crop for both frames. Caption the second “The plate sits on foam here.” Save a short press-and-release clip for when the assembled plate can be filmed.

**Can you type this without moving a hand?** A Story alongside 13. Show a blank text field and the string `()` on the first frame. Ask how people enter a pair of brackets on their own board. Follow with a single overhead take of someone typing the pair on Hojicha44, with the text field inset. Name the layer actually used in the take.

**What does this dial do today?** After 14, film three identical turns of the same dial with the screen in frame. Cut from the volume indicator to a scrolling page to the next track, keeping the layer-holding hand visible. Use the working firmware and capture the real screen response. End with the most interesting dial binding someone suggested on 14, with permission to quote them.
