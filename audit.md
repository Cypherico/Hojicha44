Three physical problems affect both halves. The PJ-320A jack cannot seat in the released holes, the JST battery header intersects the plate and cover, and the stock power-switch leads extend into the tub floor. The switch has a dimensioned prototype trim procedure. The two connectors still require a board or enclosure revision. ## 1. Layout and ergonomics

- The live layout is [Ergogen config](layout/ergogen/config.yaml) and its generated points. The `layout/- FINAL/` files are historical snapshots. The 44 switches are 6×3 fingers and four 1u thumbs per half, with the two encoders as separate inputs. The matrix centres are 19.00 apart, with column stagger and no finger-column splay. The paired outlines and switch geometry are mirrored, including the widened split. The electrical layout uses outer-to-inner ordering on each half, so right-hand matrix indices increase from the user's right towards the centre.
- **The physical layout is logical for its stated intent.** Letter placement follows conventional QWERTY. The extra inner keys expose punctuation, the two momentary layers are within the thumb sweep, and number and navigation layers make sense on a 44-key board. The four-thumb arc and the measured cap spacing are viable as geometry. Actual hand size, pinky stretch to the outer column, comfortable hold positions, left/right separation and negative tilt still need a 1:1 hand test. The documented neutral thumb arc is a design assumption.
- Oriented rectangles from both released plate DXFs yield 1.52 minimum cap-to-cap gap with nominal 17.48×16.48 KLP caps, 1.00 with 18×17 MBK-size caps, and 0.50 with 18.5×17.5 caps. 19×19 caps touch in the finger field. Sweeping all 44 rectangular cap footprints through the full height of the actual covers gave zero intersections on both halves. The minimum cap-to-cover gap was 1.293 for KLP and 0.925 for MBK-size caps, including the rotated thumbs. Qualify the selected cap through full switch travel and lateral wobble, including the rotated thumb corners and the closest cap to each encoder knob.
- The underlying L1 and L2 bug was independent of PCB routing. The indexed right hand was populated as if indices increased left-to-right. On the reviewed build the physical first row was `1 2 3 4 5 6 | = - 0 9 8 7`. Positions matched the generated files and compiled, which explains why the existing static gate never failed.
- Hold-tap uses balanced, 150 ms prior idle, 280 ms tapping and opposite-hand position lists. Reasonable for roll suppression, with a responsiveness tradeoff for single-hand shortcuts. Test fast alternating-hand rolls, same-hand GUI/Ctrl shortcuts, modifier combinations, repeated letters, and the two-layer chord. The guide's example treating O as left-hand was incorrect, and its physical base diagram printed the right half in firmware order.

- `check_board.py --self-test` and the v0.5.1 release manifest pass. ### PJ-320A and the frame conversion

The jack defect lives in the physical board, independently of the cable or firmware. The release places the left footprint origin at `(122.45, −22)`. Excellon slot centres for the three inline contacts are `(125.65, −24.30)`, `(128.65, −24.30)`, `(132.65, −24.30)`. The lone contact is `(133.75, −19.70)`. The right has the opposite global facing and the same underlying local reflection. The stock jack pattern has two locating pins 7.0 apart and an asymmetric longitudinal sequence of contacts. Reflecting the local pattern changes its handedness, so rotating the jack on the top side cannot align all its terminals and pegs.

The vendor drawing puts the plug face **2.0 before the local zero**, adjacent to the first locating post. The two pegs are 3.6 and 10.6 behind the face. The four contacts are approximately 5.2, 8.2, 12.2 and 13.4 behind the face. A cylindrical-feature inspection of the vendored STEP independently places its open end at raw STEP z=+2, with the remaining axial extent going to −12.1. That corresponds to the footprint's **local y=−2**, where the footprint silk shows the nose. The assumed y=+12.1 in `case/params.py` is near the opposite end. The package cannot be salvaged by moving the case hole alone. [XKB PJ-320A drawing](https://uploadcdn.oneyac.com/attachments/files/brand_pdf/xkbconnectivity/PJ-320A.pdf) · [Placed footprints](pcb/generators/gen_pcb.py) · [Left Excellon](generated/release/v0.5.1/Left/Hojicha44-Left.drl)

The XKB mounting layout specifies Ø1.2 locating holes for nominal Ø0.75 posts, four 0.5×1.5 terminal openings and a 13.2 dimension to the lone contact. The component side view implies 13.4 to that contact. The carrier uses Ø0.8 locating holes and 0.9×1.3 oval terminal slots. For the revision use the mounting drawing, check slot end radii and plating with the fab, and check one actual jack. The part drawing shows terminal projections of about 1.6–1.8 below the body, leaving little lead beyond a 1.6 PCB for inspection. [XKB mounting and component drawings](https://uploadcdn.oneyac.com/attachments/files/brand_pdf/xkbconnectivity/PJ-320A.pdf)

The release converts only the Choc footprint from native KiCad local coordinates before reflecting the full board. The EC11 signal drilling is symmetric, so the reflection swaps the physical A/B labels relative to the stock footprint. Both halves have the same orientation. The five Ø1.0 signal holes match the vendor's 2.5 pin spacing and 7.5/7.0 row offsets from the shaft. The carrier has two round Ø2.6 anchor holes 11.2 apart. The rigid lugs intersect a model of the 1.6 PCB by 0.048 and 0.046 mm³ per half. The fit of the real sprung metal tabs needs a coupon. Qualify insertion and retention on the intended 1.6 PCB. [Alps mounting drawing 2](https://tech.alpsalpine.com/cms.media/product_catalog_ec_01_ec11e_en_611f078659.pdf)

**PCB revision decision.** The simpler BLE shipping design can omit the two jacks and replace external TRRS with labelled GND/TX/RX test pads if debug is required. Retaining external TRRS means using a top-view footprint keyed to the real drawing, fixing the opening direction, rechecking pad-to-contact identities with a fully seated plug, rerouting both boards and making a shorter or properly counterbored plug path. Add per-part signed placement/drill checks with a negative control for mirrored patterns. The firmware currently carries keystrokes between halves over BLE even when the left half uses USB HID. The left overlay enables the UART hardware, the right leaves it disabled, and there is no configured TRRS split transport. Bare TTL UART is a bench/debug interface. The present jack exposes MCU GPIO directly and a TRRS plug wipes across contacts during insertion. Retaining an externally accessible interface warrants review of series/ESD protection and contact sequencing.

### Battery, charger and switch

- Pack+ → switch common → nice!nano RAW/B+ with pack− → GND is a sound topology for the genuine module. The pack can charge only with the switch **ON**. USB can still power the controller with the switch **OFF**. The right firmware disables USB HID, with the module USB still serving charging and bootloader functions. The boost jumper must remain open. Confirm the pack polarity by meter at the mated harness and by continuity all the way to controller RAW/GND before connecting a pack.
- A 110 mAh pack at 100 mA is approximately 0.91C. The named reseller describes charging it on a nice!nano but provides no pack manufacturer charge limit, cell/PCM tolerance or swelling allowance. `301230` is a form factor. Another vendor's protected 301230 is specified for only 70 mA. Obtain the actual pack data and verify a current-limited charge cycle, peak current, termination and rest voltage with the exact module. The time-to-charge includes a CV taper. The series PPTC in the BOM is loose harness hardware. Specify its part and physical position so it cannot bear on the pouch, and confirm the circuit is protected against the intended fault. [nice!nano charger](https://nicekeyboards.com/docs/nice-nano/) · [Specified battery listing](https://kriscables.com/product/rechargeable-110mah-li-po-battery-301230-jst-ph/) · [EEMB 301230 example](https://jm.pl/gfx-base/s_1/orgs/18/LP301230-PCM-LD-EEMB.pdf)
- With 15.24 between pin rows and a 12.0 pouch there is 3.24 gross width for **both** rows of pin/socket intrusions, pouch tolerance and insulation, approximately 1.62 per side before those are deducted. A 4.5 mm mated under-module stack leaves nominally 1.5 above a bare 3.0 pouch. Include the PCM seam and tape, solder, cell tolerance, pigtail exit and a positive retention/abrasion barrier with no compression on the cell. Measure the installed pack and actual available envelope. Keep metallic foil and the harness out of the antenna's near field where practical and measure BLE range with the closed case. The carrier's copper keepout does not qualify the antenna and battery together.
- The named Mill-Max 315-43-112-41-003000 strip specifies 0.071 inch (1.80 mm) insulation height. Its 3320-0 mating pin is straight and has no shoulder that fixes the board gap. The supplier offers an alternate Samtec BBL-112-T-E header with more height and no published installed dimension for this stack. Qualify the actual mated pair and minimum engagement, and measure from carrier PCB top to controller underside. The earlier BOM's 3.0 mm socket body claim and the pin-tip-to-mating-face measurement are unsuitable fabrication datums. [Mill-Max socket drawing](https://static6.arrow.com/aropdfconversion/2e225be7d9212620894362dafa7ce735623fe9ca/315-43-112-41-003000.pdf) · [Mill-Max straight pin drawing](https://datasheet.octopart.com/3320-0-00-15-00-00-03-0-Mill-Max-datasheet-180682269.pdf) · [splitkb kit options](https://splitkb.com/collections/all-products/products/mill-max-low-profile-sockets)

With that pin order, the keyed S2B-PH-K-S body lies towards **negative assembly Y**, spanning y=0.75–8.35. The top cover's MCU opening starts at y=12.0 and its encoder opening ends at −1.65. ## 3. Case, gasket, plate and assembly

### Suspension and materials

- Each half is a tub and a flush cover, with eight 10×5 plate tabs in pockets. A pair of 7×4 PORON pads on each tab supports the plate and attached switch/PCB assembly. The bottom shelf is z=−1.55, plate nominal z=1.00–2.20, and cover seat z=3.20. The stack has 4.75 available for 1.20 plate plus 4.5 free foam, or **0.95 mm total squeeze** before adhesive thickness. The case sets total squeeze. The stated 15% bottom / 33% top distribution assumes the PCB is held at z=0. For identical pad material and area, ignoring gravity, equal forces imply roughly equal strain at **21.1%**, shifting the PCB down about **0.18 mm**. As-built adhesive, foam density and thickness, hard contacts, weight and plate flex alter that result. Measure static PCB/port z and travel under a firm key/encoder press before freezing the case and padding.
- The BOM should pin actual foam series, density, nominal thickness and adhesive backing. Rogers' current 4701-30 guide shows the 240 kg/m³ variant beginning at **4.78** and 320 kg/m³ beginning at **1.57** in the listed unsupported ranges. The old 240 kg/m³ 3.0 mm callout does not follow that table. A 1.57 commercial sheet may be an option after recalculating the stack. At ±10% sheet thickness, the nominal foam stack can span 4.05–4.95, giving roughly **12–28% total compression** at the same case and plate dimensions. The 0.707 pad-corner to plate-edge clearance passes the existing 0.60 geometry floor, with 0.107 margin. Verify cutting, attachment and full tab support on an assembled coupon. Adhesive should hold the foam on the tab without bonding it to the case. [Rogers PORON 4701-30 product data](https://www.rogerscorp.com/elastomeric-material-solutions/poron-industrial-polyurethanes/poron-4701-30)
- **Plate.** 1.2 PC is a reasonable compliant prototype choice with Choc v1 clips and the existing 14.0 square cutouts. Qualify the finished holes and stock thickness with the actual switches. Laser through-cutting alone cannot make the 22 controlled-depth, 3.15×2.00 underside reliefs. A supplier must machine them and confirm the process on a coupon. The nominal 0.55 relief gives 0.65 remaining material and 0.15 clearance above the modeled 1.40 worst assembled diode, giving **zero surplus** against the 0.15 acceptance criterion. With a 0.60 residual floor the nominal feasible depth interval is 0.55–0.60, before plate/PCB standoff and part variation. The preferred robust revision is to remove top-side diode interference, for example by checking B.Cu diode placement together with socket clearance and rerouting. A qualified lower-profile package with suitable pads is another option. Aluminum is stiffer and easier to machine locally, with antenna and solder insulation rechecks. POM flexes differently and has poor adhesive bonding without a suitable treatment. Acrylic is a poor choice for these thin tab roots.
- **Tub and cover.** MJF PA12 is a sensible production candidate, with a 1.70 floor slab, ~2.6 tub wall and 2.0 nominal cover. The nominal assembly is about 166.9×131.0×11.55 per half, excluding caps, feet and knobs. The cover pockets leave 1.30 behind the magnets and the TRRS bore leaves only 0.70 at its roof before edge effects. Thin, wide parts and tight port features need print flatness, wall and XY shrink checks. PETG is useful for a fit article, with print direction and warpage affecting strength and dimensions. Bulk material tensile figures and 100% infill are insufficient to establish pull strength. Confirm case feet, desk grip and flatness on both halves. [HP MJF design guidance](https://www.hp.com/us-en/printers/3d-printers/learning-center/how-to-design-3d-print-model.html) · [JLC MJF constraints](https://jlc3dp.com/3d-printing/multi-jet-fusion/)
- **Cover retention.** Eight axial Ø4×2 tub magnets pair with eight Ø4×0.5 cover magnets per half. Ø4.3 pockets, depths 2.2/0.7, leave nominal radially 0.15 and axially 0.2 for adhesive. The seated case faces establish the cover stop provided magnet or adhesive does not sit proud. No screws or positive lateral registration are present. Verify paired polarity, seating flatness, peel, lateral slip and repeatable removal while foam is installed. The existing 1.5 kg pull test measures axial retention only. Long-term cover creep and magnet adhesion to actual finished PA12 or PETG need test. Consider hidden locating features if the cover shifts enough to rub caps, the knob or power control.
- **Half-to-half attachment.** The left and right assembled keyboards have no mechanical bridge, hinge, latch or magnet pair across the split. Their relative position is free on the desk. The magnetic joint is cover-to-tub on each side. The intended functional link is BLE. At the drawn mirror spacing, the case envelopes come within about **2.11** at their closest point. The legacy layout gap numbers measure PCB/jack positions before cases are fitted.

### Floor and optional foam

At nominal z the floor top is −4.65, the 1.60 PCB bottom is −1.60, and the bare-board cavity is **3.05**. The vendored Choc socket model reaches −3.468 (**1.18** free). Kailh dimensions the housing at 1.80±0.10 deep and shows 3.05 overall including the posts that enter the PCB. The former 1.25 underside projection understated the housing. The encoder tips reach −3.65 (**1.00** free), JST tips −3.40 (**1.25** free) and the vendored jack model −3.00 (**1.65** free). The stock power switch is the limiting part. [Kailh socket drawing](https://www.kailhswitch.com/Content/upload/pdf/202115927/CPG135001S30-data-sheet.pdf)

**The Würth power-switch leads hit the floor.** The drawing dimensions the lead from the seating foot at **5.00 +0.10/−0.30**. This puts even the shortest untrimmed lead 0.05 into the floor at the nominal PCB plane. At nominal lead length the interference is **0.35** and at the long limit it is **0.45**. A Ø0.60×5.00 cylinder at each of the six actual lead positions independently intersected solid tub material. For the existing case, the first-article assembly procedure trims each soldered lead and verifies the lowest point of lead or solder is at most **3.65 from PCB top**. This recovers 1.00 nominal clearance at the switch. For full-length leads, a floor at or below −6.10 there would accommodate the long-limit part and that same 1.00 reserve, before allowing for gasket position. [Würth drawing, page 1](https://www.we-online.com/components/products/datasheet/450301014042.pdf)

After resolving the lead collision, try **0.5–1.0 floor-adhered, nonconductive acoustic foam in open zones**, relieved around the encoder, power switch, connector legs, supports and sockets as needed. A continuous 1.0 sheet would already fill the nominal clearance under the encoder. Measure the actual solder and clearance with no foam first, then repeat with foam through full working travel. Foam touching the PCB would carry load and change the gasket feel.

## 4. Firmware and release workflow

- Upstream ZMK is pinned to `edf5c0814fd3ea202e43aad2d68fd32e882a518c`. The 48 transform positions are 44 switches, two reserved left thumb positions and two encoder pushes. The right transform offsets columns by six. Composite scan supplies the push row. P0.10 is configured as GPIO by the UICR overlay. Verify that **stock bootloader** flashing really enables encoder push and preserves recoverability. The encoder driver at this pin forwards peripheral sensor events over BLE. `steps=60` and `triggers-per-rotation=30` are consistent with four quadrature edges × 15 pulse periods. Actual phase, bounce and skipped detents need bench validation. Both encoder pushes map to Mute, and rotation maps to volume, scroll or track depending on layer.
- The central handles USB HID and BLE host output, fetches peripheral battery level and proxies it. The peripheral has BLE and no application USB HID. Host exposure of the second battery percentage is host-dependent. Test simultaneous charging and USB typing, host endpoint choice while charging, disconnect/reconnect with either half restarted, BLE profiles, range and both battery readings under load. The scanned firmware has **deep sleep disabled**. Measure idle current, real runtime of the central, reconnect time and wake behavior before changing power defaults. [ZMK split settings](https://zmk.dev/docs/config/split) · [ZMK power settings](https://zmk.dev/docs/config/power)
- Encoder A/B and push use the nRF52840's internal pull-ups. At an assumed 3.3 V and typical 13 kΩ, a continuously closed contact draws about **0.25 mA**, below the encoder's specified 1 mA minimum resistive operating current. Alps also gives a 500 µA at 5 V minimum for the push switch. Measure idle current at different detents, false or skipped counts, and contact behavior on the chosen parts. A stronger pull-up changes both switching current and battery life. [Nordic GPIO electrical specification](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52840/page/gpio.html) · [Alps electrical ratings](https://tech.alpsalpine.com/e/products/detail/EC11E15244G1/)
- The Adjust bootloader/reset bindings are on left W/E. A disconnected or incorrectly flashed right half needs independent recovery. Prove reach of RST/GND with the cover removed, document stock reset entry, and publish a `settings_reset` UF2 or a repeatable build for it. A normal UF2 flash preserves BLE bonds. Keep the reset process for both half roles. [ZMK pairing recovery](https://zmk.dev/docs/troubleshooting/connection-issues)
- Five BLE profiles, OS-specific clipboard shortcuts and accessible home-row modifiers are well matched to the intended compact board. Shifted legends and clipboard macros assume the corresponding host keyboard/application conventions. Check GUI/Ctrl on the chosen OS, clipboard macros, cross-hand chords, all 44 switches plus both pushes, all layers, and encoder events in both directions.
- The active CI build covers both halves, while variant CI compiles the left role for all four variants. Local audit builds use the same pinned ZMK checkout and an ARM GNU 13.2 toolchain. For any pilot, record keymap variant, revision, build/toolchain, and the two UF2 hashes. The variant container's `stable` tag is movable. A PCB release version alone does not identify firmware. Future engineering should bind the PCB, plate/STEP, BOM and firmware to a common release record.
- Print manifest hashes match the supplied solids and plate files. The PCB manifest hashes match the current PCB bytes. Several order documents reference an older board, wall or magnet revision. Specifically, package correlation and physical hand checks need authority beyond a hash and a regression gate. `case/probe_bores.py` had legacy tub USB probes at z=4.5 and 5.95 above the current z=3.20 lip. An empty point above the tub passes an air probe without exercising the bore. The mirror gate also reads `case/out` directly even when generator `CASE_OUT` is set.
- The repository records a personal-build scope. If that changes, collect evidence for the exact module revision and determine the finished product's applicable radio, EMC, and Bluetooth qualification path. The older "pre-certified module" language in the order guide was unsupported. [Project compliance record](spec/compliance.md) · [ACMA product evidence](https://www.acma.gov.au/step-2-show-your-product-complies) · [Bluetooth SIG qualification](https://www.bluetooth.com/develop-with-bluetooth/qualify/)

## 5. Recommended sequence to first article

1. Decide whether to retain the external wired debug jack. Correct or depopulate its footprint and resolve the case port accordingly. Resolve JST orientation and service access with the actual housing, harness and controller stack. Re-export and compare released drill, lands, nets and component envelopes in one top-side assembly frame.
2. Qualify the chosen exact protected battery, mated socket height, physical fuse and power switch continuity. Use a current-limited source for unpowered-to-powered bring-up. Confirm charge behavior before enclosing the cell.
3. Build a plate coupon covering the Choc cutout, diode underside relief and encoder opening. Measure clip retention, plate residual thickness, actual diode clearance and all occupied regions. Use case slices to test USB, JST, power actuator, encoder push and TRRS if retained.
4. Assemble one complete pair. Inspect actual cap travel, static and pressed PCB height, closed-case gasket contact, floor/foam freedom, cover shear and peel, jack/USB reach, and battery serviceability. Verify the 44 key and two encoder input sheet, pairing/recovery, current and charging.
5. Cut a coordinated release and use the first-article evidence to decide batch fabrication. Historical releases remain traceable by hash.
